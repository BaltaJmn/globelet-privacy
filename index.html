GLOBELET

Wrap the world into a ball.

Globelet is an iPhone app that bends a picture until the ground curls into a planet and the sky wraps around it. It works on photos and panoramas, and on video: every frame of a clip becomes the planet, with its sound.

What it does

Drag around the planet to turn it, pinch to resize it, flip it into a tunnel with the sky in the middle. Save a photo, or let the planet turn and save that as a video or a GIF.

Pictures and video from a 360 camera wrap exactly and can be tilted.

Where your pictures go

Nowhere. Everything is made on the iPhone; there is no account and nothing is uploaded. The details are on the privacy page.

Help

Something not working, or a picture that will not open? Write to baltajmn@gmail.com and say which iPhone you have and what kind of picture it was.
